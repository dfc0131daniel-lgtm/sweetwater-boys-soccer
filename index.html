About the Program

Competing with heart, pride, and purpose.

The Sweetwater High School Boys Soccer program develops skilled, confident athletes who compete with character and class. From the first training session to league play, our players are challenged to improve every day and represent our school with pride.

We value work ethic, leadership, and a team-first mentality that sets the standard both on and off the field.
